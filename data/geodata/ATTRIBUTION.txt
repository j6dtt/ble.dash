cities.csv is derived from the GeoNames geographical database
(https://www.geonames.org/), specifically the "cities5000" export
(all cities with a population >= 5000, or capitals of administrative
divisions), trimmed to name/lat/lon/country/admin1/population. The
`admin1` column (state/province/governorate — whatever a given country's
top administrative subdivision is) is resolved from GeoNames' separate
admin1CodesASCII.txt mapping (country.code -> name) and left blank for
places with no such subdivision (e.g. city-states like Singapore).
